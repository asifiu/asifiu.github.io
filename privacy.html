Privacy

Datenschutzerklärung · last updated 17 October 2026

Who is responsible

Asif Iqbal, Berlin, Germany. Email: asifiqbal.eee@gmail.com.

In short

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. I count visits anonymously to know whether the portfolio is read and which CV people download. I cannot see who you are.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver a page, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and the usual browser details, and GitHub may log them to keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, delivering the site safely.

Visit counts (GoatCounter)

I use GoatCounter, run by Martin Tournoij (Ireland) on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland. It counts page views and clicks on the CV downloads and contact links.

  • No cookies, no local storage, no identifier is placed in your browser.
  • Your IP address and the full browser identification are not stored. The IP address is used briefly to work out the country and to recognise a repeat visit for up to 8 hours, then discarded.
  • Only totals are kept: page, day, browser and system, country, language, screen width and the site you came from.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my interest in knowing whether the portfolio is useful for my job search. You can block the counting with any content blocker; the site works the same. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

Email

If you email me, I use your message and address only to reply and for the application process, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
