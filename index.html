Projects

Early detection of Parkinson's disease from voice and spiral drawings

2026

M.Sc. thesis, IU International University of Applied Sciences Python

A reproducible Python pipeline for two kinds of clinical data, 195 voice recordings and hand-drawn spiral images, from cleaning to modelling to the final figures.

The most useful result was about the data, not the model. The 195 recordings came from only 32 people, so a random split put the same person in both training and test data. I measured the effect: random splitting made the AUC look 0.21 better on average.

  • Compared eight voice models with participant-level cross-validation and chose one by a fixed rule.
  • Combined XGBoost on voice with DenseNet121 on images; explained the decisions with SHAP and Grad-CAM.
  • 30 automated checks tie every number in the thesis to the data.

pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow/Keras, SHAP, statsmodels, matplotlib
