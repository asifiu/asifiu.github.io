Work I can show you

M.Sc. thesis · IU International University · 2026

Early detection of Parkinson's disease from voice and spiral drawings

A reproducible Python pipeline for two kinds of clinical data: 195 voice recordings and hand-drawn spiral images, from cleaning to modelling to the final figures.

The most useful finding was about the data, not the model. The 195 recordings came from only 32 people, so a random split put the same person in training and test data. I measured the effect: random splitting made the AUC look 0.21 better on average.
  • Compared eight voice models with participant-level cross-validation and chose by a fixed rule
  • Combined the voice model (XGBoost) with the image model (DenseNet121)
  • Explained the decisions with SHAP and Grad-CAM
  • 30 automated checks tie every number in the thesis to the data
